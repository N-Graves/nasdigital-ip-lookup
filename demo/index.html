The data centre is not where you are

The three-letter code is an airport code for the Cloudflare data centre that answered this request — the nearest one with capacity, which is usually but not always near you. It says more about how your traffic is routed than about your address.

Where the numbers come from

Cloudflare already sits in front of this site and already knows all of this, because it handled the request. The page asks it directly rather than sending your address off to a third-party geolocation API, which is what most "what's my IP" pages do — and which means handing your address to a company you did not choose so they can tell you what it is.

Nothing here is stored. There is no database, no log of visitors, and the page has no way to remember you were here.

How accurate is the location?

The country is generally right. Anything more precise is estimated from which network block your address sits in, and it is routinely wrong by tens of miles — it often points at wherever your provider registered the range rather than at you. Treat it as a rough hint, not a location.
